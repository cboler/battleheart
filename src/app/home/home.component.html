❤️ Tribute, Credits & Preservation Disclaimer

This project is an unofficial, non-commercial fan recreation of the original Battleheart (2011), created by Mika Mobile (Noah Bordner & Kelly Reagan). All credit for the original game concept, class mechanics, and tactical line-drawing RPG magic belongs to Mika Mobile.

If you enjoy their work, please support Mika Mobile directly by purchasing Battleheart Legacy on Steam and checking out their other titles!

🛡️ Why This Exists (Game Preservation & Safety)

Because the original 2011 Battleheart is no longer maintained or available on modern Google Play devices, searching for ways to replay it with family today mostly leads to sketchy, malware-ridden third-party APK download sites.

This clean-room web recreation—built from scratch with original TypeScript code, synthesized Web Audio, and custom placeholder SVG graphics—was made purely out of father-and-son nostalgia and a love for preserving classic games so they can be played safely in a browser without risking device security.

⚖️ Non-Commercial & Takedown Policy

This project is 100% free and non-commercial: there are no ads, no monetization, no tracking, and no original binary assets or code from Mika Mobile are distributed here.

We respect the original creators completely and are glad to modify, rename, or take this repository down immediately if requested by Mika Mobile or the rights holders.
